Zuppa Toscana (Olive Garden Copycat)

By the Hearthside Kitchen team · Updated November 13, 2026

Copycat Olive Garden Zuppa Toscana: a creamy soup with Italian sausage, potatoes, bacon, and kale, ready in under an hour.

🔥 Viral
🔥 Why it went viral

This homemade version of Olive Garden's famous sausage, potato, and kale soup is one of the most-searched copycat recipes in America.

Prep15 min
Cook35 min
Total50 min
Serves6
Jump to Recipe

Olive Garden’s Zuppa Toscana has a devoted following, and copycat versions of it are some of the most popular soup recipes online. Good news: it is easy to make at home, and homemade tastes even better.

Spicy Italian sausage, tender potatoes, crispy bacon, and dark leafy kale simmer in a savory broth finished with a splash of cream. It is hearty enough for dinner and perfect on a cold evening with a piece of crusty bread.

6 servings

Ingredients

Instructions

  1. In a large pot over medium heat, cook the bacon until crisp, 6 to 8 minutes. Remove with a slotted spoon and set aside.
  2. Add the sausage to the pot and cook, breaking it up, until browned, 7 to 8 minutes. Remove with a slotted spoon, leaving about 1 tablespoon of fat in the pot.
  3. Add the onion and cook until soft, about 5 minutes. Add the garlic and cook 30 seconds.
  4. Pour in the broth and add the potatoes and red pepper flakes. Bring to a boil, then reduce the heat and simmer until the potatoes are tender, about 15 minutes.
  5. Return the sausage to the pot and stir in the kale. Simmer until the kale is tender, 3 to 4 minutes.
  6. Reduce the heat to low and stir in the cream. Heat through without boiling. Season with salt and pepper and top each bowl with bacon and Parmesan.

Tips for success

  • Use hot Italian sausage for an authentic kick, or mild if you prefer gentle flavor.
  • Spinach can replace the kale. Stir it in at the very end since it wilts in seconds.

Storing and reheating

Refrigerate for up to 4 days. Reheat gently on the stove without boiling. Potatoes can turn grainy when frozen, so this soup is best enjoyed fresh.
