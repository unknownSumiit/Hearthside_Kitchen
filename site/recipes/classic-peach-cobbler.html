Classic Southern Peach Cobbler

By the Hearthside Kitchen team · Updated November 13, 2026

Easy Southern peach cobbler with juicy cinnamon peaches and a buttery, golden batter crust. Works with fresh, frozen, or canned peaches.

Prep15 min
Cook45 min
Total1 hr
Serves8
Jump to Recipe

This is the peach cobbler of church potlucks and family reunions across the South. A simple batter is poured over melted butter, topped with sweet cinnamon peaches, and baked until the batter rises up around the fruit into a golden, buttery crust.

The method might seem unusual the first time you try it, but trust the process and do not stir. It works with fresh peaches in summer and frozen or canned peaches the rest of the year.

8 servings

Ingredients

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 350°F. Put the butter in a 9x13-inch baking dish and place it in the oven to melt while it heats, about 5 minutes. Watch so it does not burn.
  2. In a medium bowl, whisk together the flour, 1 cup of sugar, the baking powder, and salt. Whisk in the milk and vanilla until smooth.
  3. In another bowl, toss the peaches with 1/4 cup of sugar and the cinnamon.
  4. Remove the dish from the oven. Pour the batter evenly over the melted butter. Do not stir.
  5. Spoon the peaches and their juices evenly over the batter. Again, do not stir.
  6. Bake for 40 to 45 minutes, until the top is golden brown and the batter has risen around the peaches.
  7. Let cool for 15 minutes before serving. It is wonderful with vanilla ice cream.

Tips for success

  • To peel fresh peaches easily, drop them into boiling water for 30 to 60 seconds, then into ice water. The skins slip right off.
  • If using canned peaches packed in syrup, reduce the 1/4 cup of sugar for the peaches to 2 tablespoons.

Storing and reheating

Cover and refrigerate leftovers for up to 4 days. Reheat individual servings in the microwave for 30 to 45 seconds.
