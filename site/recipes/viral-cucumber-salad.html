Viral Cucumber Salad

By the Hearthside Kitchen team · Updated November 13, 2026

The viral shake-in-a-jar cucumber salad with soy sauce, rice vinegar, sesame oil, and garlic. Crunchy, refreshing, and ready in 10 minutes.

🔥 Viral
🔥 Why it went viral

In 2024 a creator’s “sometimes you need to eat an entire cucumber” videos sparked a nationwide cucumber craze, and this shake-in-a-jar salad was the star.

Prep10 min
Total10 min
Serves2
Jump to Recipe

In 2024, a series of short videos about eating an entire cucumber in one sitting turned into a nationwide craze, and stores in some areas even ran low on cucumbers. The idea is simple and delicious: slice a whole cucumber into a jar, add a savory dressing, close the lid, and shake.

The result is a crunchy, refreshing salad with garlicky, tangy, slightly sweet flavors. It makes a great light lunch, an easy side for grilled meats, or a healthy snack.

Viral Cucumber Salad

Batch size
2 servings

When you change the batch size, pan sizes and cooking times may need adjusting.

Ingredients

Instructions

  1. Slice the cucumber as thinly as you can with a sharp knife, or use a mandoline slicer with its hand guard.
  2. Place the slices in a large jar or a container with a tight-fitting lid.
  3. Add the soy sauce, rice vinegar, sesame oil, honey, garlic, and chili crisp.
  4. Close the lid tightly and shake well until every slice is coated.
  5. Sprinkle with sesame seeds and green onion and enjoy right away.

Tips for success

  • Always use the hand guard with a mandoline. The blades are extremely sharp.
  • English and Persian cucumbers have thin skins and small seeds, so there is no need to peel them.

Storing and reheating

This salad is best eaten fresh. It keeps in the refrigerator for 1 day but will soften and release liquid.
