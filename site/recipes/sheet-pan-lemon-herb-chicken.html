Sheet Pan Lemon Herb Chicken and Vegetables

By the Hearthside Kitchen team · Updated November 13, 2026

An easy one-pan dinner of juicy lemon herb chicken thighs roasted with potatoes, carrots, and red onion.

🍗
Prep15 min
Cook40 min
Total55 min
Serves4
Jump to Recipe

Sheet pan dinners are a gift to anyone who loves a home-cooked meal but not a sink full of dishes. Everything roasts together on one pan, and the chicken juices season the vegetables as they cook.

This recipe uses boneless chicken thighs, which stay juicy and forgiving even if they cook a few minutes longer than planned. The potatoes and carrots get a head start in the oven so that everything finishes at the same time.

Sheet Pan Lemon Herb Chicken and Vegetables

Batch size
4 servings

When you change the batch size, pan sizes and cooking times may need adjusting.

Ingredients

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 425°F. Line a large rimmed baking sheet with foil or parchment paper.
  2. On the baking sheet, toss the potatoes, carrots, and onion with 2 tablespoons of the oil, half of the salt, and half of the pepper. Spread them in a single layer and roast for 15 minutes.
  3. Meanwhile, in a medium bowl, stir together the remaining 1 tablespoon of oil, the lemon zest, 2 tablespoons of the lemon juice, garlic, oregano, thyme, paprika, and the remaining salt and pepper. Add the chicken and turn to coat.
  4. Remove the pan from the oven. Stir the vegetables and push them to the edges, then lay the chicken thighs in the center.
  5. Roast for 20 to 25 minutes more, until the chicken reaches 165°F inside and the potatoes are tender and golden.
  6. Drizzle with the remaining lemon juice and sprinkle with parsley before serving.

Tips for success

  • Give the vegetables room. If the pan is crowded they will steam instead of browning, so use two pans if needed.
  • You can use chicken breasts instead of thighs. Cut them in half horizontally so they cook evenly and do not dry out.
  • Swap in other sturdy vegetables such as sweet potatoes or Brussels sprouts.

Storing and reheating

Refrigerate leftovers for up to 4 days. Reheat in a 375°F oven or toaster oven for about 10 minutes to keep the potatoes crisp, or in the microwave until hot.
