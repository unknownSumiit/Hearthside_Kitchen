Moist Banana Bread

By the Hearthside Kitchen team · Updated November 13, 2026

A simple, one-bowl banana bread that is moist, tender, and full of banana flavor. The best use for overripe bananas.

🍌
Prep15 min
Cook1 hr
Total1 hr 15 min
Makes10 slices
Jump to Recipe

Every kitchen needs a dependable banana bread recipe, and this is it. It comes together in one bowl with no mixer, and it is the perfect way to use those bananas on the counter that have turned brown and spotty.

The riper the bananas, the better the bread. Bananas with lots of brown spots are sweeter and more flavorful, and they mash easily into the batter. Brown sugar adds moisture and a hint of caramel flavor.

Batch size
10 slices

Ingredients

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 350°F. Butter a 9x5-inch loaf pan.
  2. In a large bowl, mash the bananas with a fork until mostly smooth.
  3. Stir in the melted butter, then the brown sugar, egg, and vanilla.
  4. Sprinkle the baking soda, salt, and cinnamon over the mixture and stir well.
  5. Add the flour and stir just until no dry streaks remain. Fold in the nuts, if using.
  6. Pour the batter into the pan. Bake for 55 to 65 minutes, until a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean or with a few moist crumbs. If the top browns too quickly, cover loosely with foil after 40 minutes.
  7. Cool in the pan for 10 minutes, then turn out onto a wire rack to cool completely before slicing.

Tips for success

  • Freeze overripe bananas in their peels until you are ready to bake. Thaw them in a bowl; they will look dark and soft, which is perfect for banana bread.
  • Stir in 1/2 cup of chocolate chips with the flour for a treat the grandchildren will love.

Storing and reheating

Wrap tightly and keep at room temperature for up to 3 days or in the refrigerator for up to a week. Freeze the whole loaf or individual slices, wrapped well, for up to 3 months.
