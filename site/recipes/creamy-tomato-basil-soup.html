Creamy Tomato Basil Soup

By the Hearthside Kitchen team · Updated November 13, 2026

Smooth, creamy tomato basil soup made with canned tomatoes in 40 minutes. Perfect with a grilled cheese sandwich.

🍅
Prep10 min
Cook30 min
Total40 min
Serves6
Jump to Recipe

Tomato soup and a grilled cheese sandwich is one of the great American lunches. This homemade version is richer and brighter than anything from a can, yet it uses ingredients you probably already have in the pantry.

Good-quality canned whole tomatoes are the key. They are picked and packed at peak ripeness, so they give you summer flavor any time of year. A small spoonful of sugar balances their acidity, and a splash of cream makes the soup velvety smooth.

Batch size
6 servings

Ingredients

Instructions

  1. Melt the butter with the olive oil in a large pot over medium heat. Add the onion and cook until soft and translucent, 5 to 7 minutes. Add the garlic and cook for 30 seconds.
  2. Add the tomatoes with their juices, the broth, sugar, salt, and pepper. Break up the tomatoes with a wooden spoon.
  3. Bring to a boil, then reduce the heat and simmer, uncovered, for 20 minutes, stirring occasionally.
  4. Remove the pot from the heat. Purée the soup until smooth with an immersion blender. If using a regular blender, work in small batches, fill the jar no more than halfway, and remove the center cap of the lid and cover the opening with a folded towel so steam can escape.
  5. Return the soup to low heat. Stir in the cream and basil and heat through without letting it boil. Taste and adjust the seasoning.

Tips for success

  • An immersion (stick) blender is the safest and easiest way to purée hot soup. It is a worthwhile kitchen tool if you make soup often.
  • For a dairy-free version, use olive oil instead of butter and replace the cream with canned coconut milk.

Storing and reheating

Refrigerate for up to 5 days. For freezing, make the soup through step 4, freeze for up to 3 months, and stir in the cream and basil when you reheat it.
