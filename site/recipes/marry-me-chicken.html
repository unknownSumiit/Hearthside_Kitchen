Marry Me Chicken

By the Hearthside Kitchen team · Updated November 13, 2026

The famous viral Marry Me Chicken: golden chicken in a creamy Parmesan sauce with sun-dried tomatoes and garlic, ready in 35 minutes.

🔥 Viral
🔥 Why it went viral

Shared millions of times online, this creamy skillet chicken earned its name from people who claimed it was so good it led to marriage proposals.

Prep10 min
Cook25 min
Total35 min
Serves4
Jump to Recipe

Few recipes have taken over the internet quite like Marry Me Chicken. The story goes that the dish is so good, whoever you cook it for will propose on the spot. Whether or not that is true, it is easy to see why millions of home cooks have made it.

Tender chicken cutlets are seared until golden, then simmered in a silky sauce of cream, Parmesan, garlic, and sun-dried tomatoes. It all happens in one skillet in about half an hour, and it tastes like something from a nice Italian restaurant.

Serve it over pasta, rice, or mashed potatoes, with crusty bread for the extra sauce.

Marry Me Chicken

Batch size
4 servings

When you change the batch size, pan sizes and cooking times may need adjusting.

Ingredients

Instructions

  1. Slice each chicken breast in half horizontally to make 4 thin cutlets. Pat dry and season with 3/4 teaspoon of the salt, the pepper, and the Italian seasoning.
  2. Heat the oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add the chicken and cook until golden brown and cooked through (165°F inside), 4 to 5 minutes per side. Transfer to a plate.
  3. Reduce the heat to medium and add the butter to the skillet. Add the garlic and cook for 30 seconds. Stir in the sun-dried tomatoes and red pepper flakes and cook 1 minute.
  4. Pour in the broth and scrape up the browned bits from the bottom of the pan. Stir in the cream and the remaining 1/4 teaspoon salt. Simmer gently until slightly thickened, 3 to 4 minutes.
  5. Stir in the Parmesan until melted and smooth. Return the chicken and any juices to the skillet and spoon the sauce over the top. Simmer 2 minutes to warm through.
  6. Scatter fresh basil over the top and serve.

Tips for success

  • Thin cutlets cook quickly and evenly. If you prefer, pound the breasts to an even 1/2-inch thickness instead of slicing them.
  • Use freshly grated Parmesan rather than the shaker kind, which does not melt as smoothly.
  • For a lighter sauce, use half-and-half instead of heavy cream and keep the heat low so it does not separate.

Storing and reheating

Refrigerate for up to 3 days. Reheat gently over low heat with a splash of broth or milk, stirring, until hot.
