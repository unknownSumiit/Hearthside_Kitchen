Lasagna Soup

By the Hearthside Kitchen team · Updated November 13, 2026

Viral one-pot lasagna soup with Italian sausage, tomatoes, and broken lasagna noodles, topped with a creamy ricotta and mozzarella dollop.

🔥 Viral
🔥 Why it went viral

All the flavor of lasagna in about 50 minutes, without the layering. This one-pot soup took over social media feeds as a cozy weeknight shortcut.

Prep15 min
Cook35 min
Total50 min
Serves6
Jump to Recipe

Lasagna Soup has everything people love about a lasagna, the rich meat sauce, tender noodles, and creamy cheese, made in a single pot in less than an hour. No layering, no long bake, and far fewer dishes.

Broken lasagna noodles cook right in a tomato and sausage broth, and each bowl is finished with a dollop of ricotta and mozzarella that melts into the hot soup. It is no surprise this cozy recipe spread so quickly online.

Lasagna Soup

Batch size
6 servings

Ingredients

  • For the soup
  • For the cheese topping

Instructions

  1. Heat the oil in a large pot over medium heat. Add the sausage and onion and cook, breaking up the meat, until browned, 7 to 8 minutes. Drain any excess fat.
  2. Add the garlic and cook 30 seconds. Stir in the tomato paste, oregano, basil, and red pepper flakes and cook 1 minute.
  3. Add the crushed tomatoes and broth and bring to a boil.
  4. Stir in the broken noodles. Reduce to a simmer and cook, stirring often so they do not stick, until tender, 12 to 15 minutes.
  5. Stir in the cream and Parmesan. Season with salt and pepper.
  6. In a small bowl, mix the ricotta, mozzarella, and parsley. Ladle the soup into bowls and top each with a spoonful of the cheese mixture.

Tips for success

  • The noodles keep absorbing broth as the soup sits. Add a splash of extra broth when reheating leftovers.
  • For a vegetarian version, skip the sausage, add a diced zucchini and a cup of chopped mushrooms with the onion, and use vegetable broth.

Storing and reheating

Refrigerate for up to 3 days. To freeze, make the soup without noodles and freeze for up to 3 months; cook fresh noodles in it when reheating.
