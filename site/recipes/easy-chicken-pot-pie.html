Easy Chicken Pot Pie

By the Hearthside Kitchen team · Updated November 13, 2026

Creamy, comforting chicken pot pie made easy with rotisserie chicken, frozen vegetables, and refrigerated pie crust.

🥧
Prep20 min
Cook35 min
Total1 hr 5 min
Serves6
Jump to Recipe

Chicken pot pie is the kind of dinner that makes everyone come to the table early. A creamy, savory filling of chicken and vegetables baked under a flaky golden crust is hard to beat on a chilly evening.

This version keeps all the comfort but skips the fuss. A store-bought rotisserie chicken, a bag of frozen peas and carrots, and refrigerated pie crusts turn it into a simple weeknight meal. The homemade sauce takes about 10 minutes and tastes far better than canned soup.

Batch size

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 425°F. Let the pie crusts sit at room temperature for 15 minutes so they unroll without cracking. Press one crust into a 9-inch deep-dish pie plate.
  2. Melt the butter in a large saucepan over medium heat. Add the onion and celery and cook until softened, 3 to 4 minutes.
  3. Stir in the flour, salt, pepper, and thyme and cook, stirring, for 1 minute.
  4. Slowly whisk in the broth and milk. Cook, stirring constantly, until the sauce bubbles and thickens, 3 to 5 minutes.
  5. Stir in the chicken and the frozen peas and carrots. Pour the filling into the crust.
  6. Lay the second crust over the top. Trim, fold the edges under, and crimp to seal. Cut several small slits in the top so steam can escape, and brush with the beaten egg if using.
  7. Bake for 30 to 35 minutes, until the crust is deep golden brown and the filling is bubbling. If the edges brown too quickly, cover them with strips of foil after 15 minutes.
  8. Let the pot pie rest for 10 minutes before serving so the filling can set.

Tips for success

  • Place the pie plate on a foil-lined baking sheet to catch any drips in the oven.
  • Leftover turkey works perfectly in place of chicken, which makes this a great recipe for the days after Thanksgiving.
  • To make individual pot pies, divide the filling among oven-safe ramekins and top each with a circle of crust. Bake for 20 to 25 minutes.

Storing and reheating

Refrigerate leftovers, covered, for up to 3 days. Reheat individual portions in a 350°F oven for 15 to 20 minutes to keep the crust crisp. An unbaked, assembled pot pie can be wrapped well and frozen for up to 3 months; bake from frozen at 400°F for about 60 minutes, covering the edges as needed.
