Creamy Mashed Potatoes

By the Hearthside Kitchen team · Updated November 13, 2026

Rich, fluffy, creamy mashed potatoes with butter and warm milk, plus a make-ahead tip for holidays.

🥔
Prep15 min
Cook20 min
Total35 min
Serves6
Jump to Recipe

Mashed potatoes are a must at every holiday table and a welcome side on any weeknight. Great mashed potatoes are smooth, fluffy, and buttery, never gluey or lumpy.

The type of potato matters. Yukon Golds give you naturally creamy, buttery potatoes, while russets make the fluffiest mash. Warming the milk before adding it keeps the potatoes hot and helps them absorb the butter evenly.

Batch size
6 servings

Ingredients

Instructions

  1. Place the potatoes in a large pot and cover with cold water by about 1 inch. Add 1 tablespoon of salt.
  2. Bring to a boil over high heat, then reduce to a steady simmer. Cook until a fork slides into the potatoes with no resistance, 15 to 20 minutes.
  3. Drain the potatoes well, then return them to the empty pot over low heat for 1 to 2 minutes, stirring, to let excess moisture steam off.
  4. Mash the potatoes with a potato masher or press them through a ricer.
  5. Add the butter and stir until melted. Gradually stir in the warm milk until the potatoes are as creamy as you like, then stir in the sour cream if using.
  6. Season with salt and pepper to taste and garnish with chives.

Tips for success

  • Never use a food processor or blender for mashed potatoes. The fast blades turn them gummy.
  • Starting the potatoes in cold water helps them cook evenly from the outside in.
  • Make ahead: mashed potatoes can be kept warm in a slow cooker on the LOW or WARM setting for up to 2 hours. Stir in a splash of warm milk before serving.

Storing and reheating

Refrigerate for up to 4 days. Reheat in the microwave or on the stove over low heat with a splash of milk and a pat of butter, stirring often.
