Cowboy Caviar

By the Hearthside Kitchen team · Updated November 13, 2026

Fresh, colorful cowboy caviar with black beans, black-eyed peas, corn, peppers, and a tangy lime dressing. Perfect as a dip or side salad.

🔥 Viral
🔥 Why it went viral

This colorful Texas dip dates back to the 1940s, and it found a whole new audience when it went viral on TikTok in 2022.

Prep20 min
Chill30 min
Total50 min
Serves8
Jump to Recipe

Cowboy Caviar, also called Texas Caviar, was first created in the 1940s by Helen Corbitt, a famous Dallas cook. Decades later it exploded on TikTok, and it is easy to see why: it is fresh, colorful, healthy, and endlessly scoopable.

Black beans, black-eyed peas, sweet corn, crunchy peppers, and juicy tomatoes are tossed in a bright lime and red wine vinegar dressing. Serve it with tortilla chips as a dip or spoon it beside grilled chicken or fish as a side salad.

Instructions

  1. In a large bowl, combine the black beans, black-eyed peas, corn, bell pepper, tomatoes, red onion, jalapeño, and cilantro.
  2. In a small jar or bowl, shake or whisk together all of the dressing ingredients.
  3. Pour the dressing over the salad and toss well.
  4. Cover and refrigerate for at least 30 minutes so the flavors blend.
  5. Just before serving, gently fold in the avocado. Serve with tortilla chips or as a side salad.

Tips for success

  • Rinsing canned beans removes much of the extra sodium and the starchy liquid.
  • Make it a day ahead for parties, but leave out the avocado until serving so it stays green.

Storing and reheating

Refrigerate without the avocado for up to 4 days. Stir before serving.
