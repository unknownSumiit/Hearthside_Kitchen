Viral Salmon Rice Bowl

By the Hearthside Kitchen team · Updated November 13, 2026

The viral salmon rice bowl: leftover salmon and rice warmed and mashed with soy sauce, mayo, and sriracha, topped with avocado. Ready in 10 minutes.

🔥 Viral
🔥 Why it went viral

A food creator’s quiet videos of mashing leftover salmon and rice in a bowl drew millions of views and turned leftovers into a sensation.

Prep5 min
Cook3 min
Total8 min
Serves2
Jump to Recipe

This recipe became famous through a food creator who filmed herself turning leftover salmon and rice into a quick lunch. Millions of people watched and then made it themselves.

Leftover salmon and rice are warmed together, then mashed with soy sauce, a little mayonnaise, and sriracha. Topped with avocado and eaten with crisp roasted seaweed snacks, it is savory, creamy, and satisfying. It is a wonderful way to use leftovers from our Baked Salmon with Lemon and Dill.

Viral Salmon Rice Bowl

Batch size
2 servings

When you change the batch size, pan sizes and cooking times may need adjusting.

Ingredients

Instructions

  1. Place the rice and salmon in a microwave-safe bowl. For moist rice, set an ice cube on top and cover the bowl loosely with a piece of parchment paper.
  2. Microwave for 1 1/2 to 2 minutes, until steaming hot throughout (165°F). Remove and discard what is left of the ice cube.
  3. Add the soy sauce, mayonnaise, and sriracha. Mash and stir everything together with a fork.
  4. Top with the avocado and sesame seeds. Scoop up bites with roasted seaweed snacks, or simply eat with a spoon.

Tips for success

  • Cooked rice should be cooled and refrigerated within 2 hours and eaten within 3 to 4 days. Always reheat until steaming hot.
  • Japanese-style mayonnaise is traditional, but any mayonnaise works.

Storing and reheating

Best enjoyed right away. Keep the leftover salmon and rice refrigerated separately until you are ready to make the bowl.
