Garlic Butter Steak Bites

By the Hearthside Kitchen team · Updated November 13, 2026

Juicy, tender garlic butter steak bites seared in a hot skillet in about 20 minutes. A steakhouse dinner at home.

🔥 Viral
🔥 Why it went viral

Juicy seared steak bites tossed in sizzling garlic butter became a social media favorite because they taste like a steakhouse dinner in 20 minutes.

Prep10 min
Cook10 min
Total20 min
Serves4
Jump to Recipe

Garlic butter steak bites are a steakhouse dinner made easy. Bite-size pieces of sirloin get a deep golden sear in a very hot skillet, then are tossed in melted butter and garlic right at the end.

Because the pieces are small, they cook in just a few minutes, and the whole dinner comes together in about 20. Serve them with mashed potatoes, a green salad, or roasted vegetables.

Batch size
4 servings

When you change the batch size, pan sizes and cooking times may need adjusting.

Ingredients

Instructions

  1. Pat the steak cubes very dry with paper towels and season with the salt and pepper.
  2. Heat a large cast-iron or heavy skillet over medium-high heat until very hot. Add the oil.
  3. Add the steak in a single layer, working in two batches if needed. Cook without moving for 1 to 2 minutes, until a deep brown crust forms, then turn and cook 1 to 2 minutes more for medium. Transfer each batch to a plate.
  4. Reduce the heat to medium. Return all the steak to the skillet, add the butter and garlic, and toss for about 1 minute, until the garlic is fragrant and the steak is coated.
  5. Sprinkle with parsley and serve right away.

Tips for success

  • Drying the meat and using a very hot pan are the keys to a good sear. Wet meat or a crowded pan will steam instead of brown.
  • The USDA recommends cooking steak to at least 145°F followed by a 3-minute rest.
  • Ribeye or New York strip also work beautifully if you want a richer cut.

Storing and reheating

Refrigerate for up to 3 days. Reheat briefly in a hot skillet so the steak does not overcook.
