Old-Fashioned Apple Crisp

By the Hearthside Kitchen team · Updated November 13, 2026

Warm cinnamon apple crisp with a buttery brown sugar oat topping. Easier than pie and perfect with vanilla ice cream.

🍎
Prep20 min
Cook45 min
Total1 hr 5 min
Serves8
Jump to Recipe

Apple crisp gives you everything people love about apple pie, warm cinnamon apples and a buttery topping, without any rolling or crimping of pie dough. It is one of the easiest desserts you can make, and one of the most comforting.

A mix of tart and sweet apples gives the best flavor and texture. Granny Smith apples hold their shape and add tang, while Honeycrisp, Gala, or Golden Delicious add sweetness.

Old-Fashioned Apple Crisp

Batch size
8 servings

When you change the batch size, pan sizes and cooking times may need adjusting.

Ingredients

  • For the apple filling
  • For the crisp topping

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 350°F. Butter a 9-inch square baking dish or a 2-quart baking dish.
  2. In a large bowl, toss the apple slices with the granulated sugar, flour, lemon juice, cinnamon, and nutmeg. Spread the apples evenly in the baking dish.
  3. In another bowl, stir together the flour, oats, brown sugar, cinnamon, and salt. Add the cold butter and work it in with your fingertips or a pastry blender until the mixture forms coarse, crumbly clumps.
  4. Sprinkle the topping evenly over the apples.
  5. Bake for 40 to 45 minutes, until the topping is golden brown and the juices are bubbling around the edges.
  6. Let cool for at least 15 minutes before serving. Serve warm with vanilla ice cream or whipped cream.

Tips for success

  • Set the baking dish on a foil-lined baking sheet to catch any bubbling juices.
  • Add 1/2 cup of chopped pecans or walnuts to the topping for extra crunch.
  • In summer, try a mix of peaches and blueberries instead of apples.

Storing and reheating

Cover and keep at room temperature for 1 day, or refrigerate for up to 5 days. Reheat individual portions in the microwave for 30 to 45 seconds, or warm the whole dish in a 350°F oven for 15 minutes to re-crisp the topping.
