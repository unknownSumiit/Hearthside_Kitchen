Make-Ahead Veggie Egg Muffins

By the Hearthside Kitchen team · Updated November 13, 2026

Easy baked egg muffins with spinach, peppers, and cheddar. Make a batch on Sunday for quick, protein-packed breakfasts all week.

Prep15 min
Cook22 min
Total37 min
Serves6
Jump to Recipe

Egg muffins are like little crustless quiches baked in a muffin tin. They take about 15 minutes of hands-on time, and once they are made, breakfast is ready in less than a minute for the rest of the week.

This version uses spinach, bell pepper, onion, and sharp cheddar, but the recipe is very flexible. Use whatever vegetables and cheese you have in the refrigerator. Leftover ham, cooked sausage, or crumbled bacon are all welcome additions.

Make-Ahead Veggie Egg Muffins

Batch size
6 servings (12 egg muffins)

When you change the batch size, pan sizes and cooking times may need adjusting.

Ingredients

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 350°F. Generously grease a 12-cup muffin tin with butter or cooking spray, or line it with silicone muffin cups.
  2. Heat the olive oil in a skillet over medium heat. Add the bell pepper and onion and cook until softened, about 4 minutes. Add the spinach and stir just until wilted, about 1 minute. Remove from the heat and let cool for a few minutes.
  3. In a large bowl or a large measuring cup with a spout, whisk the eggs, milk, salt, and pepper until well blended.
  4. Divide the cooked vegetables and the cheese evenly among the muffin cups. Pour the egg mixture over the top, filling each cup about three-quarters full.
  5. Bake for 20 to 25 minutes, until the centers are set and no longer jiggle.
  6. Let the muffins cool in the pan for 5 minutes, then run a thin knife around the edges to release them.

Tips for success

  • Cooking the vegetables first removes extra moisture, so the egg muffins are not watery.
  • Silicone muffin cups make removal much easier and are worth buying if you make these often.
  • For a lighter version, replace half of the whole eggs with liquid egg whites.

Storing and reheating

Refrigerate in an airtight container for up to 4 days. To freeze, wrap individually and store in a freezer bag for up to 2 months. Reheat in the microwave for 30 to 45 seconds (60 to 90 seconds from frozen) until hot.
