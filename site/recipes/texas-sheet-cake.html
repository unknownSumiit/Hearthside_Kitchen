Texas Sheet Cake

By the Hearthside Kitchen team · Updated November 13, 2026

Classic Texas sheet cake: a moist, thin chocolate cake topped with warm fudge frosting and pecans. Feeds a crowd.

🔥 Viral

A potluck legend for generations, this thin chocolate cake with warm fudgy frosting keeps going viral because it feeds a crowd and never lasts long.

Prep20 min
Cook22 min

Texas Sheet Cake has been a church supper and family reunion favorite for generations, and it keeps finding new fans online. It is a big, thin chocolate cake baked in a sheet pan and covered with a warm, fudgy pecan frosting that sets into a glossy layer.

Because it bakes in about 20 minutes and serves two dozen, it is the perfect cake for a crowd. The frosting is poured on while the cake is still warm, so it soaks in slightly and stays wonderfully moist.

Texas Sheet Cake

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 350°F. Grease an 18x13-inch rimmed half-sheet pan.
  2. In a large bowl, whisk together the flour, sugar, baking soda, and salt.
  3. In a saucepan, bring the butter, water, and cocoa to a boil, stirring. Pour over the flour mixture and whisk until smooth.
  4. In a small bowl, whisk the buttermilk, eggs, and vanilla. Whisk into the batter.
  5. Pour into the pan and spread evenly. Bake for 18 to 22 minutes, until a toothpick in the center comes out clean.
  6. During the last 5 minutes of baking, make the frosting: in a saucepan, melt the butter with the cocoa and milk and bring just to a boil. Remove from the heat and whisk in the powdered sugar and vanilla until smooth. Stir in the pecans.
  7. Pour the warm frosting over the warm cake and spread it to the edges. Let cool completely before cutting into squares.

Tips for success

  • Using a smaller 15x10-inch pan makes a thicker cake. Bake it for 25 to 30 minutes.
  • Toast the pecans in a dry skillet for 3 to 4 minutes for even more flavor.

Storing and reheating

Cover and keep at room temperature for up to 3 days, or refrigerate for up to a week. The cake freezes well, wrapped tightly, for up to 3 months.
