Homemade Chicken Noodle Soup

By the Hearthside Kitchen team · Updated November 13, 2026

Classic homemade chicken noodle soup with tender chicken, carrots, celery, and egg noodles, ready in about an hour.

🍜
Prep15 min
Cook40 min
Total55 min
Serves6
Jump to Recipe

This is the soup people crave when the weather turns cold or someone in the house is feeling under the weather. It is simple, honest cooking: tender chicken, soft carrots and celery, and wide egg noodles in a savory broth.

Using good store-bought broth keeps this recipe to about an hour from start to finish. Poaching the chicken right in the soup adds flavor to the broth and keeps the meat juicy.

If you plan to have leftovers, read the tip below about cooking the noodles separately. It makes a big difference on day two.

Homemade Chicken Noodle Soup

Batch size
6 servings

Ingredients

Instructions

  1. Heat the oil or butter in a large pot over medium heat. Add the onion, carrots, and celery and cook, stirring occasionally, until the onion is soft, 6 to 8 minutes. Add the garlic and cook 30 seconds more.
  2. Pour in the broth and add the whole chicken pieces, thyme, and bay leaf. Bring to a boil, then reduce the heat and simmer gently, partly covered, until the chicken reaches 165°F inside, 15 to 20 minutes.
  3. Remove the chicken to a cutting board. Let it cool for a few minutes, then shred it with two forks.
  4. Bring the soup back to a boil and add the noodles. Cook until tender, usually 6 to 8 minutes (check the package).
  5. Return the shredded chicken to the pot. Remove the bay leaf. Stir in the parsley and lemon juice, then season with salt and pepper to taste.

Tips for success

  • Egg noodles keep soaking up broth as they sit. If you expect leftovers, cook the noodles in a separate pot of salted water and add them to each bowl when serving.
  • Short on time? Leave the raw chicken out, simmer the broth and vegetables for 10 minutes, and stir in 3 to 4 cups of shredded rotisserie chicken after the noodles are cooked.
  • A squeeze of lemon at the end brightens the flavor without adding salt.

Storing and reheating

Refrigerate for up to 4 days. For best results, freeze the soup without noodles for up to 3 months, then add freshly cooked noodles when reheating. Reheat until steaming hot (165°F).
