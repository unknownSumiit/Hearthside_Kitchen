Old-Fashioned Stuffed Bell Peppers

By the Hearthside Kitchen team · Updated November 13, 2026

Classic stuffed bell peppers filled with seasoned ground beef, rice, and tomatoes, topped with melted cheese.

Prep20 min
Cook45 min
Total1 hr 5 min
Serves4
Jump to Recipe

Stuffed peppers are a classic American dinner that has graced family tables for generations. Each pepper is its own neat little meal: a tender shell filled with savory beef, rice, and tomatoes, finished with a blanket of melted cheese.

This recipe is a good way to use leftover rice, and the peppers can be stuffed a day ahead and baked when you are ready. Ground turkey works just as well as beef if you prefer.

Old-Fashioned Stuffed Bell Peppers

Batch size
4 servings

When you change the batch size, pan sizes and cooking times may need adjusting.

Ingredients

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 375°F. Cut the tops off the peppers and remove the seeds and white ribs. If needed, trim a thin slice from the bottoms so they stand upright (do not cut through).
  2. In a large skillet over medium heat, cook the ground beef and onion, breaking up the meat, until browned, 7 to 8 minutes. Drain off any excess fat. Add the garlic and cook for 30 seconds.
  3. Stir in the diced tomatoes, rice, half of the tomato sauce, Italian seasoning, salt, pepper, and 1/2 cup of the cheese.
  4. Stand the peppers upright in a baking dish just large enough to hold them. Fill them with the beef mixture, packing it in gently. Spoon the remaining tomato sauce over the tops. Pour 1/4 cup of water into the bottom of the dish.
  5. Cover the dish with foil and bake for 35 minutes, until the peppers are tender.
  6. Uncover, sprinkle the remaining cheese over the peppers, and bake 10 minutes more, until the cheese is melted and bubbly.

Tips for success

  • For softer peppers, boil the hollowed-out peppers for 3 minutes and drain them upside down before stuffing.
  • Red, yellow, and orange peppers are sweeter than green peppers. Use any mix you like.

Storing and reheating

Refrigerate cooked stuffed peppers for up to 4 days. Freeze them individually wrapped for up to 3 months. Reheat in the microwave or in a covered dish at 350°F until hot in the center.
