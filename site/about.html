About Us

Hearthside Kitchen is a home for comforting, dependable recipes, the kind that bring people to the table.

Our story

Many of the best meals are the simple ones: a pot of soup on a cold day, a meatloaf on Sunday, a warm apple crisp shared with friends. We started Hearthside Kitchen to collect these classic American dishes in one place and to write them the way a good friend would explain them in the kitchen, clearly, patiently, and without fuss.

What makes our recipes different

  • Everyday ingredients. Our recipes use ingredients found in any American grocery store.
  • Clear, numbered steps. We explain not just what to do but how to tell when something is done.
  • Cooking for one, two, or a crowd. Every recipe can be halved or doubled with a single tap.
  • Helpful extras. Each recipe includes tips for success and instructions for storing, freezing, and reheating leftovers.
  • Food safety first. We include safe internal temperatures and follow USDA guidance in our storage advice.

Designed for easy reading

We built Hearthside Kitchen to be comfortable to use on a phone or tablet in the kitchen. The text is large and high-contrast, buttons are big and easy to tap, and you can make the text even larger with the Text size button. On recipe pages, Cook mode keeps your screen from going dark while you cook, and you can check off ingredients as you add them.

Who we are

Hearthside Kitchen is written and maintained by the Hearthside Kitchen team, home cooks who believe a good meal is one of life’s simple pleasures. We test our recipes in ordinary home kitchens with ordinary equipment.

Advertising
