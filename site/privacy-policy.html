Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Hearthside Kitchen (“we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, and protects information when you visit https://www.yourdomain.com (the “Site”). By using the Site, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

Information we collect

Information you give us

You do not need an account or any personal information to use this Site. If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message, and we use it only to respond to you.

Information collected automatically

Recipe photos are loaded from Unsplash’s image servers, which receive your IP address and browser information when an image loads, as described in Unsplash’s privacy policy. Our hosting provider (Microsoft Azure) automatically records standard technical information when you visit, such as your IP address, browser and device type, pages visited, and the date and time of your visit. This information is used to operate, secure, and improve the Site.

Preferences stored on your device

If you change the text size, the Site saves your choice in your browser’s local storage. Ingredients you check off on a recipe are saved in session storage until you close the tab. These preferences stay on your device and are not sent to us. Cook mode uses your browser’s Screen Wake Lock feature and does not collect any information.

Cookies and similar technologies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device. We and our partners may use cookies and similar technologies to remember preferences, understand how visitors use the Site, and deliver and measure advertising. You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. On iPhone and iPad, go to Settings → Apps → Safari (or Settings → Safari on older versions) to manage cookies and website data.

Advertising and Google AdSense

We use Google AdSense and may work with other advertising partners to display ads on the Site. These partners may use cookies, web beacons, device identifiers, and similar technologies to collect information about your visits to this and other websites to provide relevant advertisements.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this Site or other websites.
  • Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this Site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google My Ad Center.
  • You can also opt out of some third-party vendors’ use of cookies for personalized advertising at www.aboutads.info/choices.
  • Learn more at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Analytics

We may use analytics services, such as Google Analytics, to understand how visitors use the Site. These services may use cookies and collect information such as your IP address and browsing activity. You can opt out of Google Analytics with the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

How we use information

  • To operate, maintain, and improve the Site
  • To respond to your messages
  • To display and measure advertising that keeps the Site free
  • To protect the Site against fraud, abuse, and security threats
  • To comply with legal obligations

We do not sell your personal information for money.

Your privacy choices and rights

Depending on where you live, including California, Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, Texas, and other U.S. states with privacy laws, you may have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information we hold about you, and to opt out of the use of your information for targeted advertising. Visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland may have similar rights and will be asked for consent before personalized advertising cookies are used.

To make a request, email us at contact@yourdomain.com. You can also limit advertising cookies at any time using the links above, your browser settings, or a browser that supports the Global Privacy Control signal.

Children’s privacy

This Site is intended for a general adult audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Links to other websites

The Site may link to other websites. We are not responsible for their privacy practices and encourage you to read their privacy policies.

Data security and retention

We use reasonable safeguards, including secure HTTPS connections. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. We keep email correspondence only as long as needed to respond and for reasonable record-keeping.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date above shows when it was last revised.

Contact us

Questions about this Privacy Policy? Email us at contact@yourdomain.com.
