Few things are as comforting as a pot of soup simmering on the stove. Soups and stews are forgiving to make, easy on the budget, and they often taste even better the next day.

Most of these recipes freeze beautifully, which makes them perfect for cooking once and enjoying several meals. Look for the storage notes at the bottom of each recipe.
