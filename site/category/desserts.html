Desserts & Baking Recipes

These are the desserts that fill the house with the smell of cinnamon and butter: fruit crisps and cobblers, a moist loaf of banana bread, and a big batch of chocolate chip cookies.

None of them needs special equipment or advanced skills. If you can stir a bowl and set a timer, you can bake every recipe on this page.
